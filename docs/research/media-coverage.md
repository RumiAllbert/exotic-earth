## Search coverage and limits

Discovery covered Rumi Allbert, Rumi Alexander Allbert, Rumi Albert, Rumi Calles, and Rumi Elías Calles, alongside terms for interviews, videos, podcasts, news, Ralston, Wolfram, micro1, and institutional affiliations. Searches also checked YouTube-indexed results and followed the college article to the official recording page. The strongest identity matches combined a name with the known college, research area, or affiliation. The short name Rumi produces many unrelated results and cannot establish identity by itself.

No additional named, standalone video interview was verified. Social-network recruiting posts, congratulations, directory listings, and academic citations were excluded from the selected set. This does not imply that unindexed videos, deleted posts, private recordings, or appearances published without a name are absent. Those would require an original link or a transcript that identifies the speaker.

For future additions, record the original publisher, publication date, exact URL, and the person's role in the item. Use labels such as Quoted, Interview, Video, Podcast, and College news only when the underlying evidence supports them. Keep one homepage entry per underlying appearance, with translations or follow-up coverage in this inventory. Do not infer endorsements, audience size, or professional outcomes from the mere existence of an article.
